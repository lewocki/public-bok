# How to rent an apartment

You will be renting for at least one year to get good price otherwise rental is considered as short term and price will be significantly higher.

1. Decide on apartment criteria (location, arrangement, price, transport) as much as you can
2. If you are not confident please contact one of real estate agencies below, otherwise you can try various aggregators and rent apartment on your own
3. Consider options and choose few good ones. Reply to agent or owner quickly, good options disappear within couple of days.
4. Arrange visits and check apartment. Ask for monthly payment, deposit (kaucja), administrative payments (czynsz), and media (gas, water, electricity). Ask for agency fee to get full price. Don't leave deposit without confirmations (receipt, contract, agreement) and check owner documents if you prefer to rent on your own. Check that everything works and there is no debt.
5.  Sign agreement and make sure you put in start date, end date, how to obtain keys, how to return deposit, all payments, way they will be made and due dates, current unility meters readings. Add move in protocol with puctures. Contact should be in Polish and if possible in English

### How to request real estate agency service

For your request to be effective, please provide the real estate agency with the following information:

- The number of people who will stay in the apartment
- Children's age, if applicable
- If you have animals / what kind
- A preferred number of rooms (given that the living room and the kitchen is one room)
- Preferred location
- If a parking space in the garage is required
- Maximum budget (given that all fees are included)
- Additional preferences (e.g. old/new building, balcony/garden, view, etc.)

### Recommendation on renting an apartment

- Preparation of the lease contract - Polish and English versions required, you should read them carefully.
- You can have comments and try to negotiate the terms of the contract
- Preparation of the moving-in protocol – which will document the condition of the apartment for the moment of your moving in.
- Detailed photo documentation is highly advisable. It should constitute an integral part of moving in the protocol.
- Utility meter readings (electricity, gas, heating, water) – so that it is possible to calculate the actual usage of the utilities at the end of the lease.

### Recommendations on lease contract

- Should be in Polish (the best option is to have it in both English and Polish version)
- Should include all family members - required for future address registration
- Should clearly state the period of rent
- Should be valid at least until end of Residence Permit process
